description: Preserve historical meaning when changing question types and future-track categories.

Do not reinterpret completed historical answers when the questionnaire changes. Both the original future tracks and the subsequent seven report-aligned tracks differ conceptually from the training-course groups. Keep historical rankings separate; resumed drafts must rank the current categories again rather than receive an inferred mapping.

**Why:** Reusing or mapping old ranking identifiers would attribute participants' past priorities to subjects they never ranked.

**How to apply:** Normalize old single-choice preferences into one-element arrays when resuming a draft, leave completed records untouched, and retain historical labels for reporting. Use separate ranking cohorts with their own scale sizes.

The user now wants the future-track ranking and the training-track selector to use the same categories and exact names.

**Why:** The user requested consistency between these two questions.

**How to apply:** Derive both questions from the training-group labels rather than maintaining separate category lists. Preserve older ranking cohorts without relabelling them as current training groups.
