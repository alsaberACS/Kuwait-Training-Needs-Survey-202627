Candidate Experience (p. 9).

METHOD

About the numbers
Every figure in this catalogue is computed from the data behind the brief and the
needs study. This page explains each indicator.
Evidence base

The Training Knowledge Hub behind this catalogue holds 82
verified research reports on workforce skills (global, Gulf and
Kuwait-specific), the published 2025-26 training plans of 11
Kuwaiti organisations (430 programmes) and a benchmark of
provider courses. The Kuwait Skills Priorities 2026 decision
brief distilled this into 50 recommended workshops with an
evidence score from 0 to 100, and shortlisted 34 workshops in
five categories, all of which appear here.

Market demand

Each workshop belongs to one of ten skill themes. Market
demand is the share of the 82 reports that identify a need in
that theme, so all workshops in a theme carry the same figure.
Workshops added from the needs study inherit the demand of
the theme they belong to; their specific demand is measured in
the study itself.

Kuwait plans

The number of programmes among the 430 Kuwaiti planned
programmes whose title relates to the workshop's topic, found
by matching Arabic and English topic keywords chosen per
workshop. A title is counted once per organisation; a
programme can relate to more than one workshop, and the full
match list is generated with the catalogue for review. The
category shares on the openers use the brief's classification of
all 430 programmes.
